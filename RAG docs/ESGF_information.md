# ESGF Knowledge Base

## Definitions:

### Definition: Earth System Grid Federation (ESGF)

The Earth System Grid Federation (ESGF) is a peer-to-peer (P2P) network and software infrastructure that supports the management, dissemination, and analysis of Earth system data. It is an international collaboration of modeling centers and research institutions, serving as a centralized platform for accessing and sharing data related to the Earth's atmosphere, oceans, land, and biosphere. ESGF plays a vital role in advancing scientific research by providing access to large volumes of model simulations and observational records.

### Definition: scenario/experiment ssp585

In CMIP6, SSP5-8.5 is a scenario representing a future with high greenhouse gas emissions and a strong reliance on fossil fuels, resulting in substantial warming by the end of the 21st century. It combines Shared Socioeconomic Pathway (SSP) 5, which outlines a world characterized by intensive fossil fuel use and development challenges, with a radiative forcing level of 8.5 watts per square meter (W/m²) by 2100. This scenario is considered to be at the upper end of the range of potential future warming outcomes.

### Definition: scenario/experiment ssp245

SSP2-4.5 (often just called SSP245) is one of the Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs) used in climate modeling to describe potential future greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and their impact on climate. It combines SSP2, a "middle-of-the-road" socioeconomic scenario, with RCP 4.5, a moderate radiative forcing pathway.
